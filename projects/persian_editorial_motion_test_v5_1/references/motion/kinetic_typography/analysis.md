# Reference Motion Analysis: Persian Kinetic Typography

## Exemplars
- High-end Iranian Documentary Titles (Simorgh Crystal awardees)
- Modern Persian Cultural Center Exhibitions
- Academic Keynote Motion Designs

## Typographic Rules for Persian Motion
1. **Zero Text-Wrap Reflow**: Kinetic containers must be sized with explicit safety bounds so animated words never hop to a new line mid-flight.
2. **Kashida & Word-Level Integrity**: Never slice individual Arabic/Persian letters arbitrarily (which destroys ligatures). Animate words or full phrases using opacity, tracking, or clip-path masks.
3. **Weight Pairing**: 
   - Headline: Vazirmatn / Dubai Black (800–900 weight)
   - Category Eyebrow: Medium (600–700 weight) with letter-spacing tracking
   - Body & Description: Regular/Medium (400–500 weight)
4. **Bilingual Subtext**: English Latin subtitles or acronyms (`IR.NREC`, `ISI/WoS`, `Q1`) are aligned cleanly to support scholarly authority without overwhelming RTL primary reading flow.

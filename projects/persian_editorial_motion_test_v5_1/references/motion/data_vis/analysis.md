# Reference Motion Analysis: Data Visualization

## Exemplars
- The Economist Graphic Detail
- Financial Times Visual Journalism
- Pudding.cool Kinetic Datasets

## Visual Language
- Gauges, dials, polar charts, and quartile blocks with explicit physical weight.
- Clear categorical chromatic mapping:
  - Clinical Ph.D.: Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`)
  - Master's: Amber (`#FBBF24`)
  - Research Ph.D.: Institutional Gold (`#D4AF37`)
  - Medicine/Dentistry: Emerald (`#10B981`)
- No gratuitous 3D pie charts or skeuomorphic clutter. Glassmorphic frosted planes with 1px border highlights.

## Value Progression & Snapping
- Numbers do not count up as continuous blur; they step through candidate or minimum milestones (e.g. `12.0 -> 14.5 -> 16.0 -> LOCK`).
- At the moment of lock, a brief micro-scale impulse (1.08x -> 1.00x) and gold glow flash cements visual authority.

## 2. ARTISTIC COMPOSITION & SCALE HIERARCHY

- **Extreme Scale Contrast:**
  - Micro contextual indicators (14–16px, 800 weight with letter-spacing tracking) paired directly with monumental graphic numerals (140px–260px).
- **Negative Space Authority:**
  - Avoided the temptation to "fill every corner". Scenes 1, 2, 3, and 5 leave expansive negative space for eye travel, producing an unmistakable high-end documentary feel.
- **Controlled Asymmetry:**
  - No default center-stacking. Key focal points are weighted along the golden ratio (1/3 or 2/3 canvas coordinates).

---

## 3. MOTION GRAMMAR & EDITORIAL TRANSITIONS

- **Elimination of Forced Universal Morphs:**
  - Replaced mechanical continuous line morphing with purposeful editorial cuts, directional mask reveals, and scale punches that respect the rhythm of speech.
- **Narrative-Driven Pacing:**
  - Stillness $\to$ anticipation $\to$ typographic impact $\to$ stationary hold for comprehension $\to$ clean editorial handoff.
- **Audio Coordination:**
  - Major impacts land on exact semantic milestones (e.g. the numeral «۱۶» hits on the spoken GPA announcement; the amber cutoff boundary triggers on «حداکثر تا یک سال پس از فراغت»).

---

## 4. CONTENT & VOICE FIDELITY

- **Canonical Script:** 100% verbatim immutability; zero word or character changes.
- **Voice Stem:** Single continuous Google Gemini-TTS speech stem (`active_narration_count = 1.0`).
- **Soundtrack & Mastering:** Integrated at $-15.2\,\text{LUFS}$ with $-14\,\text{dB}$ automated sidechain ducking.
- **Font Rendering:** Official Yekan Bakh registered across all weights; zero fallback or character distortion.

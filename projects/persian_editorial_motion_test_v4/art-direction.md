# Art Direction & Visual Identity Specification (v4.1)

## 1. High Concept: "Solemn Academic Precision"
The visual identity fuses the dignity of an Iranian national academic award with the razor-sharp kinetic elegance of high-end editorial motion graphics (as seen in Claude Opus 5.5 showcases and Vox/Bloomberg scientific briefings).

### Anti-Patterns Explicitly Banned
- ❌ **No UI/Dashboard Aesthetic**: No rounded rectangular HTML cards with simple CSS `box-shadow` floating in blank space.
- ❌ **No Slideshow Behavior**: Every transition is a kinematic handover (line carry, camera whip-pan, depth push, or vector morph).
- ❌ **No Arbitrary Camera Jitter**: Handheld camera noise is strictly prohibited. Camera motions are smooth, motivated tracks or steady pans with perspective warping.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Atmospheric Materials
- **Deep Void / Background**: `#050814` (Deep Midnight Obsidian) with an underlying procedural coordinate mesh (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.04)`).
- **Primary Institutional Gold**: `#D4AF37` (Metallic Gold) — used for emblems, focal needle indicators, and decree titles.
- **Illuminating Gold**: `#F9E79F` — for high-contrast numeric callouts and active gauge thresholds.
- **Academic Slate**: `#0F172A` / `#1E293B` — for structural geometry and background framing planes.
- **Scientific Cyan / Metric Accent**: `#38BDF8` — for secondary progress streams and data ticks.
- **Validation Emerald**: `#10B981` — for approved compliance marks and threshold met states.
- **Editorial White**: `#F8FAFC` — for primary Persian text readability.

---

## 3. Typographic System & Hierarchy
- **Persian Display**: Vazirmatn ExtraBold / Black for titles and decree articles.
- **Persian Body / Explanatory**: Vazirmatn Medium for legal requirements and criteria.
- **Data & Metric Numerals**: Tabular figures (monospace layout) with high vertical cadence.
- **Tracking Physics**: Letter spacing expansion driven by individual glyph `translateX` offsets (preventing browser layout reflow jitter).

---

## 4. Multiplane Spatial Architecture (2.5D Depth)
- **Layer 0 (Background - 0.35x parallax, 2px ambient blur)**: Sacred geometric grid, subtle astronomical/institutional coordinate lines, slow drift.
- **Layer 1 (Midground - 0.7x parallax, sharp focus)**: Primary narrative focus — ministerial seal, precision radial gauges, metric charts.
- **Layer 2 (Foreground - 1.4x parallax, 3px lens blur)**: Passing golden particles, kinetic framing lines, and lens flare sweeps.

## 2. Hard Anti-Noise Rule: The 4-Role Filter
Every graphic element rendered on screen MUST fulfill at least one of these four explicit functions:
1. **[A] Narrative:** Directly conveys canonical script meaning (titles, numbers, official terms).
2. **[B] Structural:** Visualizes relationships, hierarchy, or prerequisites (diagram branches, framing pedestals, dividers).
3. **[C] Kinetic:** Acts as a motive vehicle for state change or transition continuity (carry baseline, baton impulse).
4. **[D] Atmospheric:** Provides subtle cinematic depth without distraction (subtle architectural grid lines, faint background radial glow).

**Forbidden Elements (Strictly Blocked by Preflight):**
- English text, Latin code annotations, fake HUD telemetry (`SEC_01`, `ACCREDITATION CODE`, `TIER 01`, `STATUS: VERIFIED`).
- Random geometric debris (unmotivated floating dots, unmotivated sparks, random cyber boxes).

## 4. Voice-First Synchronization Architecture
The spoken narration stem (`public/audio/persian_editorial_v5_5/final_master_mix.wav`) is the physical timing clock.
Every acoustic event is modeled with physical anticipation, contact impact, and harmonic settle:
- `anticipation`: 4–12 frames before impact (subtle pre-charge, letter-spacing compression, scale dip).
- `contact`: frame of spoken syllable strike (maximum energy, kinetic impulse, baseline draw).
- `reaction`: 6–12 frames after impact (displacement rebound, secondary glow ring).
- `settle`: 10–20 frames (exponential damping to rest).

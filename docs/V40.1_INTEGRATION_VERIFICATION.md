## 5. Semantic Beat Timing: Clarification & Rule Revision

The previous integration report stated:
> *"visual state changes occur strictly on semantic beat boundaries."*

### The Flaw in Rigid Frame Alignment
If interpreted literally, this rule would force an anticipation dip, an acceleration curve, an impact, and a settle to all happen on the exact frame of the spoken word. That is mechanically impossible and would destroy physical motion.

### The Corrected Production Principle: Timing Windows
1. **Semantic Beats define Narrative Intent and Primary Timing Windows**, not instantaneous physics frames.
2. For an spoken emphasis word occurring at frame $F_{\text{word}}$:
   - **Anticipation Pre-Load:** May begin $10\text{–}25$ frames *before* the word (leading the beat).
   - **Launch Acceleration:** Traverses toward the peak velocity.
   - **Impact Hit (`spHit`):** Synchronizes within $\pm 2$ frames of the acoustic transient.
   - **Overshoot & Settle:** Dissipates over $15\text{–}35$ frames *after* the word.
3. **Correction to SKILL.md:** `SKILL.md` Section 1.1 has been updated to clarify that semantic beats establish **timing windows**, preserving our multi-stage physical choreography (`ANTICIPATION` $\to$ `LAUNCH` $\to$ `IMPACT` $\to$ `OVERSHOOT` $\to$ `SETTLE`).

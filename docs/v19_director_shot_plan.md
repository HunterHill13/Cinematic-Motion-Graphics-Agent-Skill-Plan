### 1.2 Motion Personalities & Phase Vocabulary
Every visual element operates under a designated temporal personality:
1. **IMPACT (2–8f)**: Slams, hits, snaps, keyword strikes, and collisions with squash/stretch recoil.
2. **ELASTIC (8–24f)**: Dynamic transitions, geometric unrolling, and overshoot settling.
3. **GLIDE (30–120f)**: Smooth camera tracking, spatial reframing, and editorial drift.
4. **BUILD (45–180f)**: Continuous path growth, progressive data diagrams, and chronological accumulation.
5. **HOLD**: Deliberate narrative pauses accompanied by living camera breath, never frozen dead pixels.
6. **RELEASE (5–30f)**: Acceleration exits, fission collapses, and spatial dispersal.

## 2. Explicit Audit: Improvements, Regressions & Unchanged Areas

### A. Improvements
1. **Physical Faceting of the Sacred Nuqteh:** Replacing the flat yellow diamond with a 4-faceted milled brass pyramidion transformed Beat 04 into the most striking still frame in the entire project.
2. **Architectural Relief of «نقطه دید»:** Building the typography with dual-contour brass bevels and casting heavy drop shadows onto the receding basalt floor created genuine spatial permanence.
3. **Mechanical Shutter Realism:** The iris aperture now features 12 overlapping brass blades with cast shadows, an engraved $360^\circ$ astrolabe dial, and a chamfered smoked quartz bezel.
4. **Horizon Materiality:** The opening line now possesses physical thickness, a specular top glint, and an underlying ambient occlusion trench shadow.
5. **Restraint in Lighting:** Replaced blurry Gaussian glow spam with crisp, directional highlights and restrained filament coronas.

### B. Regressions
- **Render Weight:** File size increased from 2.7 MB (V34) to 5.1 MB (V35) due to the higher geometric density, SVG linear/radial gradients, and multi-plane SVG filters. However, rendering completed in under 12 seconds with zero dropped frames.

### C. Unchanged Weaknesses
- **Planar Translucency:** The smoked quartz scrim in Beat 03 remains an alpha-blended geometric layer rather than a true refractive optical glass shader with chromatic distortion.
- **Micro-Surface Texture:** Surfaces are mathematically smooth and pristine; they lack tactile micro-abrasions, brushed metal grain, or organic mineral variations.

### D. New Weaknesses
- **Cropped Corner During Recoil (Frame 345):** During the explosive pullback transition, the massive diamond briefly cuts across the top-left edge of the frame at an extreme scale before resolving into the final word lockup.

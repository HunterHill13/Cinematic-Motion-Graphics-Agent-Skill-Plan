# V14 REFERENCE RULES & CINEMATIC DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Primary Philosophy
> **"The Agent may invent VISUAL FORM, but it may NOT invent CONTENT."**
> **"ADD SHAPE, NOT WORD."**

Inspired by premier motion design systems: `video-shotcraft`, `video-talkcraft`, `motion-skills`, and `remotion-skills`.

---

### RULE 1: THE "REMOVE BEFORE ADD" MANDATE
Before introducing any new visual element into a shot, the motion designer must ask:
> **"Can the existing composition communicate the core idea clearly without this element?"**
- If **YES**: Do **NOT** add it.
- If **NO**: Add the **simplest, most elegant non-textual geometric form** that solves the communication problem.

### RULE 2: TEXT IS DATA — GRAPHICS ARE DESIGN
- **Content Layer is Locked:** Words, letters, numbers, dates, and titles are immutable facts derived strictly from `V14_CONTENT_MANIFEST.md`.
- **Design Layer is Creative:** Geometry, lines, circles, panels, masks, shadows, depth planes, lighting sweeps, and camera curves may be refined and created freely to serve the story.

### RULE 3: BAN ON DECORATIVE TYPOGRAPHY
- Text is **NEVER** a decorative filler pattern.
- Low-opacity background typography, repeating word walls, faux watermarks, and ambient floating sentences are strictly forbidden.
- If a background needs visual richness, use:
  - Layered depth planes
  - Thin architectural grid lines
  - Soft volumetric light bands
  - Procedural geometric contours
  - Abstract non-verbal silhouettes

### RULE 4: NON-TEXTUAL VISUAL COMPANIONS
Every textual assertion is grounded by an architectural companion, but the companion itself does **NOT** require text:
- **Shot 01:** Quadrant corner framing brackets + golden kinetic datum ray.
- **Shot 02:** Embossed geometric medallion (abstract seal) + frosted monolith glass border.
- **Shot 03:** Tripartite architectural pillar cards + geometric status icons (checkmarks, graduation caps, research calipers).
- **Shot 04:** 12-month chronological tick ruler + luminous vertical barrier gate.
- **Shot 05:** Three rising monumental pedestals (proportional physical heights: 180px, 300px, 420px).
- **Shot 06:** Grand heraldic geometric medallion + concentric orbiting rings + laurel wreath vectors.

### RULE 5: MOTION DENSITY BUDGET
To prevent visual chaos, every shot adheres to a strict budget:
- **1 Primary Motion Event:** The hero focal transformation (e.g. title strike, barrier collision, pedestal ascension).
- **1 Secondary Motion Event:** Supporting handoff or structural unfold (e.g. underline ray travel, column cards stagger).
- **0–2 Ambient Motifs:** Subtle background lighting sweep or slow camera curve.
- **Deliberate Stillness:** Allow anticipation before motion, settle after impact, and breathing room for the viewer to absorb information.

### RULE 6: DESIGNED TIMING VS MECHANIC SYNCHRONIZATION
Acoustic synchronization must feel choreographed, not robotic:
1. **Anticipation (6–14 frames before spoken word):** Visual element gathers tension or subtly squeezes.
2. **Contact (at semantic emphasis):** High-authority kinetic impact, scale punch, or collision lock.
3. **Reaction (2–8 frames after):** Radial ripples, rebound, or light wave dispersion.
4. **Settle (12–24 frames):** Harmonic decay into dignified, crystal-clear stillness.

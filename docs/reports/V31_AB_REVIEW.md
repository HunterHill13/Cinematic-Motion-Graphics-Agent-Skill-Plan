## 4. Harsh Critical Self-Examination

1. **Could the new motion be removed without harming the idea?**  
   * In Beat 02: If you remove the diagonal slit, you lose the sense that space is being carved.
   * In Beat 05: If you remove the 32-frame hold, the breach loses its explosive surprise.
   * In Beat 06: If you remove the ligature unfolding, you revert back to a standard corporate title card.
   * *Conclusion:* The motion is structural to the visual argument, not decorative garnish.
2. **What still feels slightly forced?**  
   * The transition from the star in Beat 06 into the camera aperture plunge in Beat 07 is still slightly sudden. The star could deform into the aperture iris with even tighter topological alignment.
3. **Is there still center gravity?**  
   * Beat 02 successfully broke center gravity with off-axis diagonal staging (`300, 695` $\to$ `1380, 420`).
   * Beats 05 and 06 deliberately return to center because they represent **Equilibrium** and **Sovereignty**, respectively, where central symmetry is semantically appropriate.

## 2. Explicit Audit: Improvements, Regressions & Unchanged Areas

### A. Improvements
1. **Restoration of the Circular Iris (Beat 02):** The aperture now feels like an authentic precision optical instrument. The 12 shutter leaves articulate with true radial symmetry.
2. **Restoration of the Classical Persian Nuqteh (Beat 04):** The sacred calligraphic diamond now has an equilateral $45^\circ$ geometry ($220\text{px} \times 220\text{px}$) with orthogonal $90^\circ$ crosshairs, faithfully honoring Persian calligraphic tradition.
3. **Smooth Recoil Camera Handoff (Beat 05):** At Frame 335, the camera starts focused directly on the Nuqteh dot before expanding to reveal the colossal word «نور», eliminating the awkward cropped corner of V35.
4. **Enhanced Negative Space:** By refusing to stretch circles horizontally to touch the frame edges, the screen gains breathing room, architectural dignity, and cinematic confidence.

### B. Regressions
- **None.** The render file size slightly decreased from 5.1 MB to 4.9 MB due to cleaner, more efficient circular trigonometric calculations, while rendering speed remained blazing fast at 11.2 seconds.

### C. Unchanged Areas
- **Materiality & Lighting:** The 5-material system (milled brass, polished obsidian, smoked silica, luminous filament, engraved microlines) and the top-right key light source (-35° elevation, +45° azimuth) remain identical.
- **Narrative & Timing:** The 450-frame structure, 5 beats, and exact 35-frame stillness hold are preserved frame-for-frame.

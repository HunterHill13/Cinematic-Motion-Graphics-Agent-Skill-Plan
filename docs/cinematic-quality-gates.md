# Cinematic Quality Gates & Anti-HTML Verification Protocol (v3.2)

No video will be marked production-ready based on code compilation alone. The actual rendered MP4 and audio stem mix must pass this binary checklist.

---

## 1. Anti-HTML & Web UI Elimination Gate (Hard Blocker)

- [ ] **Zero Web Containers:** Does the shot contain rectangular boxes with `border-radius: 12-24px`, background fills, or card borders? (If YES $\implies$ **REJECT / FAIL**).
- [ ] **Pure Typographic Editorial:** Do titles and subtitles float freely with clean kinetic tracking, glow drop-shadows, and zero background panels?
- [ ] **Vector Illustration vs. UI Pills:** Are scientific annotations styled as vector laser callouts with elbow hairpins and target reticles rather than pill-shaped badges?
- [ ] **Layered 2.5D Depth:** Does the composition separate into distinct spatial planes ($Z0$ atmospheric nebula, $Z1$ particles, $Z2$ secondary cristae, $Z3$ hero organelle, $Z4$ projectile, $Z5$ kinetic typography)?

---

## 2. Motion Design & Animation Gate

- [ ] **Multi-Property Entrances:** No elements allowed to enter via simple `opacity 0 -> 1`. Must combine scale, anticipation recoil, spring overshoot, and position displacement.
- [ ] **Secondary Motion during Holds:** Zero frozen frames. All elements must maintain organic breathing ($1.000 \to 1.018$) and ambient particle drift.
- [ ] **Motivated Camera Movement:** Camera tracking must follow narrative focus (e.g. following the projectile vector and punching in upon impact with $18\text{px}$ impulse shake).
- [ ] **Zero Accidental Hard Cuts:** Scene boundaries must hand off a physical anchor (`VisualMotifCore`) or use continuous optical morphs.

---

## 3. Audio & Voice Gate

- [ ] **Native Tehran Documentary Delivery:** Speech must be warm, confident, articulate, and completely free of flat eastern/Dari vowel drag.
- [ ] **Triple-Layer Pronunciation Verification:**
  - `visible_text`: Clean Arabic/Persian script without phonetic clutter.
  - `spoken_text`: Natural spoken script.
  - `phonetic_text`: Targeted diacritics applied strictly where mispronunciation occurs.
- [ ] **Dynamic Musical Scoring:** Music must have a structured emotional arc (Stasis $\to$ Build $\to$ Tension $\to$ Climax $\to$ Release) and duck by $-15\text{dB}$ under voice narration with zero mid-sentence cuts.

# Music Selection & Algorithmic Scoring Protocol (v3.2)

Generic looping background tracks are prohibited. The Music Director must match the cinematic science visual language through dynamic scoring.

---


## 2. Emotional Narrative Arc (30s Timeline)
1. **0.0s – 7.5s (Intro / Stasis):** Low sub-drone (43.6 Hz), minimal tension, establish microscopic atmosphere.
2. **7.5s – 15.0s (Build / Attack):** Filter cutoff opens, rising 16th-note arpeggiated drive, momentum gathers.
3. **15.0s – 22.5s (Tension / Rupture):** Stereo choir pad expands, percussive sub-drops on membrane puncture.
4. **22.5s – 30.0s (Climax / Execution):** Full harmonic crescendo, sweeping analog lead, climactic sub boom, gentle reverb tail decay.

## 3. Dynamic Sidechain Ducking
- Under active voiceover: duck background bed by $-14.5\text{dB}$ to $-15.0\text{dB}$.
- During narrative pauses & impact events: music lifts smoothly by $+4.0\text{dB}$ to reinforce the visual climax.

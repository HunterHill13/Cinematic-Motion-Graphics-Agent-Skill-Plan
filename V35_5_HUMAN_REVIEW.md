# V35.5 Human Design & Geometric Review

This document provides a candid human-perspective evaluation answering the 13 required questions of the V35.5 milestone.

---

## 1. Which shapes were accidentally distorted?
1. **The Ocular Iris Aperture in Beat 02:** In V35, the aperture was rendered with `rx = 320` and `ry = 180` (an aspect ratio of $1.778 : 1$). The pupil, outer chassis, smoked quartz bezel, and astrolabe dial ticks were all squashed into horizontal ellipses.
2. **The Sacred Nuqteh Diamond in Beat 04:** In V35, the diamond vertices were `(0, -110), (143, 0), (0, 110), (-143, 0)` ($110 \times 1.3 = 143$), distorting the classical $45^\circ$ equilateral square into a wide kite ($1.30 : 1$).
3. **The Recoil Camera Frame in Beat 05:** At Frame 345, the pullback camera was centered at $(CX, CY)$ while the diamond was placed at $(-140, -110)$, causing the diamond facet to crop awkwardly against the top-left corner of the frame.

---

## 2. Why were they distorted?
They were distorted as a **quick composition workaround to fill the 16:9 widescreen canvas**:
- The developer wanted the iris to occupy horizontal screen width without overflowing vertically, so they squashed it into a $16:9$ oval.
- The developer wanted the diamond in Beat 04 to look "broader" on a desktop screen, so they multiplied the X-coordinates by $1.3$.
In both cases, **geometric identity was sacrificed for screen coverage**.

---

## 3. Which shapes were intentionally deformed?
- **The Pre-Recoil Diamond (Frames 321–335):** In the 14 frames immediately preceding the explosive $10,000\times$ launch, the diamond squashes along its vertical axis ($S_Y \approx 0.92, S_X \approx 1.04$) while vibrating with harmonic tension. This is an authentic motion-design coiling behavior (spring compression before kinetic release), not accidental deformation.
- **The Sagging Horizon (Frames 28–58):** The horizontal line bows and sags into a deep catenary curve (+245px) under simulated gravitational pull.

---

## 4. Which shapes were corrected?
- **The Ocular Iris:** Restored to a strict mathematical circle ($rx = ry = 260\text{px}$, aspect ratio $1.000$).
- **The Shutter Blades & Dial:** 12 leaves and 36 degree ticks restored to true radial trigonometric symmetry.
- **The Sacred Nuqteh:** Restored to an exact equilateral $45^\circ$ diamond ($220\text{px} \times 220\text{px}$) with orthogonal $90^\circ$ crosshairs.
- **The Recoil Camera Pivot:** Re-centered dynamically on the Nuqteh dot during the start of Beat 05, eliminating awkward off-axis cropping.

---

## 5. Did camera framing replace unnecessary stretching?
**Yes.** Instead of flattening the circle into an ellipse to occupy more horizontal space, the circular aperture was sized to $520\text{px}$ diameter, allowing the top and bottom of the frame to breathe with $280\text{px}$ of rich obsidian negative space. When the camera breaches the aperture, uniform $18\times$ zoom expands the circle past the frame boundaries naturally.

---

## 6. Does the geometry now feel more intentional?
**Tremendously so.** The circular aperture now genuinely reads as a high-precision mechanical shutter or astronomical astrolabe lens. The Nuqteh diamond looks like a sacred, mathematically immaculate calligraphic jewel rather than an arbitrary four-sided polygon.

---

## 7. Does any shape still look unnaturally stretched?
**No.** All circles, rings, diamonds, squares, and radial arrays now maintain exact geometric aspect ratios. The typography retains its native font metrics.

---

## 8. Does typography preserve its proportions?
**Yes.** Both «نقطه دید» and «نور» are rendered with uniform scaling, native glyph geometry, and stroke-origin bevels. No non-uniform horizontal stretching was applied.

---

## 9. Did any correction damage the motion?
**No.** All keyframe timings, acceleration curves, anticipation offsets, viscous damping factors, and the 35-frame stillness hold remain identical to V34 and V35.

---

## 10. Did any correction damage the composition?
**No; it elevated the composition.** By removing the squashed oval, the composition gained architectural restraint, breathing room, and classical symmetry.

---

## 11. Which frames are strongest?
1. **Frame 320 (Still 320):** The sacred stillness hold. The equilateral $45^\circ$ 4-faceted brass Nuqteh suspended in vast, pristine negative space is now geometrically flawless.
2. **Frame 120 (Still 120):** The circular iris bloom. The 12 symmetrical brass blades unfurling within the circular quartz bezel look like a master watchmaker's mechanism.
3. **Frame 420 (Still 420):** The final lockup of «نور» with concentric circular astrolabe orbits.

---

## 12. Which frames still need work?
- **Frame 025 (Still 025):** The horizon pre-bow; still relatively minimal as a static still, though kinetically crucial.
- **Frame 165 (Still 165):** The entry into Beat 03; while uniform, the perspective vanishing point of the floor slabs is quite steep.

---

## 13. What remains before V36?
The project now possesses **master-tier motion choreography (V34)**, **cohesive art direction and materiality (V35)**, and **flawless geometric integrity (V35.5)**. The remaining frontier is **micro-atmospheric texture and volumetric lighting depth** (such as dynamic light shafts cutting through the circular aperture as it opens).

# Storyboard Direction C: Modern Data-Driven Kinetic Editorial

**Art Direction:** Rapid information density, stark monochrome slate (`#09090b`) with hyper-saturated electric cyan (`#06b6d4`), monospace metrics, strict tabular layouts, and fast rhythmic cuts.

* **Key Difference:** Prioritizes numerical data visualization and timeline progression over institutional insignia.
* **Transition Philosophy:** Fast horizontal match-move tracking and geometric grid reconfigurations.

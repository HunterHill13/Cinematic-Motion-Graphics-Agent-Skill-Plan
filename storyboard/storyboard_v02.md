# Storyboard Direction B: Premium Institutional & Ministerial

**Art Direction:** Emphasizes national regulatory authority, dark emerald slate (`#02120e`) with royal gold embellishments, parchment-thin dividers, and classical Persian typography flourishes.

* **Key Difference:** Focuses heavily on the ministerial decree, seal certification motifs, and official gazette aesthetics rather than modern cybernetic lines.
* **Transition Philosophy:** Architectural column wipe and dimensional book-fold reveals.

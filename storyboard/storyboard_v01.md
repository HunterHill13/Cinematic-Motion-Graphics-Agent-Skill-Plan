# Storyboard Direction A: Editorial Scientific Documentary (Selected Primary Direction)

**Art Direction:** Clean architectural glassmorphism, dignified deep navy (`#040711`), gold institutional crests (`#D4AF37`), cyan data highway (`#38BDF8`), and crisp Yekan Bakh typography with motivated object-morph continuity.

---

### Scene 1: Baqiyatallah Institutional Hook & National Research Question (0–380f)
* **Objective:** Establish university authority and present the pivotal student researcher question.
* **Composition:** Top-to-center aligned institutional crest and clean headline typography; generous negative margins.
* **Primary Element:** Rotating gold seal (frames 0–145) transitioning into national question card (frames 150–335).
* **Camera:** Static identity (`zoom: 1.0`), micro-push to `1.025` at question lock (frame 200).
* **Transition Out:** Central circular emblem collapses into horizontal cyan vector beam (frames 335–380).

---

### Scene 2: Directive Framework Decree (Band Kaf, Article 2) (350–650f)
* **Objective:** Announce the Ministry of Health decree framework as the scoring pathway.
* **Composition:** Eyebrow tag at top, central 3-node vector progression highway (`1600×360px`).
* **Primary Element:** Glowing energy beam progressing across nodes (Start -> Decree -> Recognition).
* **Transition Out:** The 3 highway nodes stretch vertically into monolith foundations (frames 620–650).

---

### Scene 3: Three Fundamental Prerequisites (620–1490f)
* **Objective:** Explain the 3 strict entrance rules: GPA $\ge 16$, disciplinary approval, and diversity across 6 articles.
* **Composition:** 3 tall architectural monolith cards side-by-side with distinct colored accent caps (Cyan, Gold, Emerald).
* **Primary Element:** Staggered card reveals synced to narration; holding steady during narration intake.
* **Transition Out:** Cards 1 & 2 fade, while Card 3 slides to the center to form the Time Gate (frames 1460–1490).

---

### Scene 4: Legal Eligibility Time Window (1460–1730f)
* **Objective:** Clarify the 1-year post-graduation deadline and during-study qualification.
* **Composition:** Centered time-window panel with dual milestone node track (Student Period <---> Max 1 Year Post-Graduation).
* **Primary Element:** Amber stopwatch badge with glowing timeline connector.
* **Transition Out:** Calendar panel compresses horizontally and fans out into 3 threshold gauges (frames 1700–1730).

---

### Scene 5: Passing Threshold Gauges by University Tier (1700–2185f)
* **Objective:** Display the exact passing points: 65 (Bachelor's Tier 1), 110 (General Medicine), 130 (Ph.D.).
* **Composition:** 3 quantitative cards with massive 64px monospace numbers and animated horizontal bar gauges.
* **Primary Element:** Number counters counting up to threshold values with zero clipping.
* **Transition Out:** 3 metric cards converge horizontally to center, condensing into the closing crest (frames 2155–2185).

---

### Scene 6: Outro Call-to-Action & Series Continuation (2155–2361f)
* **Objective:** Sign-off with Baqiyatallah PR branding and forward pointer to upcoming episodes.
* **Composition:** Centered golden seal with emerald core and two-line bold closing declaration.
* **Motion:** Dignified settle with fading soundtrack decay.

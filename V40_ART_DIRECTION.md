## 2. MATERIALITY & SURFACE PHYSICS

The Monolith and its derivative mechanisms are built from four distinct physical materials:

| Material Name | Hex / Gradient | Physical Attribute | Visual Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Milled Aerospace Titanium** | `#222A38` to `#2C374A` | Anisotropic brushed metal, 45° specular reflection, hard beveled edges | Forms the primary structural casing of the monolith, plinths, and caliper arms. |
| **Cadmium Emissive Core** | `#FF5500` / `rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.85)` | Self-illuminating, cast-light capability, volumetric bloom | Sits inside mechanical joints; expands and pulses during energetic transformations. |
| **Amber Calibration Telemetry** | `#F59E0B` / `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.9)` | Sharp vector lines, etched precision markings, laser datums | Used for dimensional measurement axes, threshold limit stops, and legal milestones. |
| **Deep Slate Reflection Floor** | Linear gradient `#0B0E14` to `#06080B` | Low-roughness Fresnel reflection floor | Grounds the objects in physical reality with dynamic contact shadows. |

## 3. LIGHTING LANGUAGE

1. **Directional Key Light:** Located at top-left-front ($(-400, -800, 600)$), casting sharp, defined 45-degree cast-shadows downward to the right.
2. **Grazing Rim Light:** Located high behind the monolith ($(+600, -600, -400)$), creating crisp, thin specular highlights along outer beveled silhouettes to separate forms from the obsidian background.
3. **Internal Core Bloom:** The cadmium core emits true radial light, illuminating adjacent titanium inner walls with warm orange bounce.
4. **Zero Ambient Washes:** No muddy global ambient fills; high dynamic range and stark value contrast ensure immediate visual readability.

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY AS PHYSICAL ARCHITECTURE

Typography in V40 is treated as a physical material intrinsic to the architectural environment, rather than a floating subtitle overlay:

```text
               INCORRECT (V39)                           CORRECT (V40)
       ┌──────────────────────────────┐          ┌──────────────────────────────┐
       │ ┌──────────────────────────┐ │          │        TITANIUM CASING       │
       │ │   Floating Card Header   │ │          │  ┌────────────────────────┐  │
       │ │ ⚠️ متن در کادر شناور ⚠️ │ │          │  │ [ ENGRAVED NUMERAL 16 ]│  │
       │ └──────────────────────────┘ │          │  │ Backlit by amber laser │  │
       │         Empty Void           │          │  └────────────────────────┘  │
       └──────────────────────────────┘          └──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Typographic Principles:
1. **Engraved & Embossed Type:** Primary numerals (**۱۶**, **۶**, **۶۵**, **۱۱۰**, **۱۳۰**) are physically cut into the titanium faces or formed directly by the negative space of the monolith's geometry.
2. **Zero Pronunciation Diacritics:** 100% of visible text is strictly run through `sanitizeForDisplay()`. Zero harakat (fatha, kasra, damma, shadda) appear in the visual render.
3. **Persian Typography Excellence:** Typeset in `Vazirmatn` with rigorous attention to Persian ligature preservation, zero subpixel jitter, and correct zero-width non-joiners (ZWNJ).
4. **Zero Unicode Emojis:** Absolute prohibition of `⏳`, `✓`, `⚠️` or unicode dingbats. Any verification mark is an authored vector signet or mechanical limit notch.

## 5. GEOMETRIC INTEGRITY

1. **True Circles:** A circle never deforms into an unmotivated oval or ellipse simply to fill rectangular canvas space.
2. **Proportional Extrusion:** Height-to-width ratios on plinths adhere to the golden ratio ($\phi \approx 1.618$) or harmonic musical ratios (1:2, 2:3, 3:4).
3. **Mass Conservation:** When the monolith splits or reconfigures, the sum volume of parts reflects the mass of the parent entity ($V_1 + V_2 \approx V_{\text{parent}}$).

# MEDICAL & SCIENTIFIC MODE

## Fundamental Principle
> **ACCURACY > CINEMATIC BEAUTY.**
> A gorgeous animation depicting an incorrect biological or biochemical mechanism
> is an academic and educational failure.

## 1. Non-Negotiable Rules
1. **Never Invent Mechanisms**: Biochemical pathways, protein conformations, and drug interactions must reflect established peer-reviewed literature.
2. **Strict Source Traceability**: Every factual claim, number, and pathway arrow must be logged in `research/sources.md` with PubMed ID, DOI, or textbook citation.
3. **Rigorous Terminology**: Consistent gene nomenclature (e.g. *TP53*, *BCL2*, *BAX*). Do not alternate between colloquial and formal names without explanation.
4. **Metaphor vs Literal Distinctions**: If a visual metaphor is employed (e.g. a "molecular scissor" for Cas9), state clearly in narration or on screen that it is conceptual.
5. **Accurate Stoichiometry & Relationships**: Up-regulation vs down-regulation, phosphorylation vs dephosphorylation, agonist vs antagonist must be indicated with proper signs (+, -, inhibitory bars).

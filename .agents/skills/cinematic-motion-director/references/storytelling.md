# STORY ARCHITECTURE & NARRATIVE ARCS

## 1. Narrative Beat Breakdown
Great motion graphics do not merely present facts; they unfold a narrative journey.
The default 7-beat structure:

```text
1. HOOK (0–10% duration)
   Bold visual question, unexpected paradox, or tension. Immediate movement within the first 15 frames.

2. PROBLEM / PARADOX (10–25% duration)
   Why the current situation fails, the biological challenge, or the technological barrier.

3. SETUP / ANATOMY (25–40% duration)
   Introducing the players, structural components, or molecular environment.

4. DEVELOPMENT & MECHANISM (40–60% duration)
   Step-by-step causal chain. A causes B, triggering C.

5. ESCALATION / THE CRISIS (60–75% duration)
   The point of highest tension or irreversible consequence (e.g. Mitochondrial pore formation).

6. REVELATION / PAYOFF (75–90% duration)
   The biological or technological breakthrough. The key metric or transformation.

7. RESOLUTION & CTA (90–100% duration)
   Final high-energy synthesis ("Launch event family photo"), takeaway conclusion, and calm hold.
```

## 2. Topic-Specific Arc Adaptation
- **Scientific/Medical**: Phenomenon -> Molecular Mechanism -> Pathological Outcome -> Therapeutic Vector.
- **Product Launch**: Problem Friction -> Paradigm Shift -> Product Reveal -> 3 Key Capabilities -> Call to Action.
- **Documentary**: Historical Mystery -> Forgotten Clue -> Technological Deep-Dive -> Modern Synthesis.

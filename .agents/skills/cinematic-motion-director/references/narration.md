# NARRATION DIRECTOR & TIMING BUDGETS

## 1. Principles of Spoken Narration
1. **Never Describe What the Viewer Already Sees**: Narration provides context, causality, and significance; the visual shows the entity and action.
2. **Natural Spoken Cadence**: Write for the vocal tract, not the printed page. Avoid tongue-twisting subordinate clauses.
3. **Pacing and Density**:
   - English standard pace: 130–155 words per minute (~2.2 to 2.6 words/sec).
   - Dense scientific explanation: 120–135 words per minute (~2.0 to 2.2 words/sec).
   - Persian (fa) standard pace: ~3.5 to 4.2 words/sec.
4. **Punctuation Controls Airflow**:
   - Paragraph breaks (`\n\n`) represent scene/shot endings and generate an extra 20–30 frames of breathing silence.
   - Commas and periods allow micro-pauses for visual landing.
5. **Hold Budget (The 30-Frame Rule)**:
   - Elements must settle and hold completely still for 30–45 frames (1.0 to 1.5s) at the end of a narrative beat before transitioning.
   - Never cut on the exact final syllable; allow the meaning to register.

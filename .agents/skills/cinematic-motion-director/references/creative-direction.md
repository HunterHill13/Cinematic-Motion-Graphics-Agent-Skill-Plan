# CREATIVE DIRECTION & STYLE SPECIFICATION

## 1. The Role of the Creative Director
Before any code or React components are written, the Creative Director establishes the visual identity, pacing, and tone of the production.

## 2. Creative Brief Dimensions
Every project must be defined across 10 core dimensions in `production/creative-brief.yaml`:
1. **Title & Logline**: Clear, compelling working title.
2. **Objective**: What the viewer should understand, feel, or do after watching.
3. **Audience**: Knowledge level (Layman, Undergraduate, Clinical/Specialist, Executive).
4. **Platform & Aspect Ratio**:
   - `16:9` (1920x1080): YouTube, Desktop, TV, Presentation, Academic.
   - `9:16` (1080x1920): TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
   - `1:1` (1080x1080): LinkedIn, Twitter, Feed Cards.
5. **Target Duration & FPS**: 30 FPS standard; duration determines pacing budget.
6. **Tone & Genre**: Scientific Documentary, Kinetic Tech Explainer, Premium Minimalist, etc.
7. **Color Palette Preset**: Guided by the 60/30/10 principle (60% background/base, 30% surface/secondary, 10% hero focal accent).
8. **Typography Pairing**: Hero display typeface (weight ≥600) + clean legible sans body font.
9. **Information Density**: Pacing budget (calm vs high-energy).
10. **Emotional Arc**: Tension -> Intrigue -> Escalation -> Revelation -> Resolution.

## 3. Style Lock Protocol
Once the brief is approved, the visual style is locked in `production/style-lock.yaml`.
No shot component may deviate from the locked palette, font pairings, or corner radiuses.
Consistency across shots is non-negotiable.

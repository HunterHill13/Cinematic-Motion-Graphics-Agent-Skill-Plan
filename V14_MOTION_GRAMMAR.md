### 1. SINGLE-CURVE CAMERA GRAMMAR
In V14, every shot is executed through exactly one primary camera curve:
- **`micro-push` ($1.000 \to 1.025$):** Used in Shot 01 to build narrative anticipation and intimacy without disorienting the viewer.
- **`slow-dolly` ($1.000 \to 1.015$):** Used in Shot 02 and Shot 04 to convey legal dignity, deliberate precision, and monumental weight.
- **`parallax-drift` ($\Delta x = \pm 12\text{px}$, zoom $1.000 \to 1.020$):** Used in Shot 03 to allow sequential scanning across 3 columns while maintaining architectural depth.
- **`continuous` ($cy: 550 \to 530$, zoom $1.000 \to 1.025$):** Used in Shot 05 to follow the rising elevation of the 3 score plinths.
- **`micro-pull` ($1.025 \to 1.000$):** Used in Shot 06 to provide a dignified institutional release and final resolution freeze.

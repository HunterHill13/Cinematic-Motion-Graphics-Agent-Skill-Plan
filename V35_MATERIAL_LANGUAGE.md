# V35 Material Language & Physical Surface Architecture

To eliminate the "flat vector on dark canvas" feel without resorting to cheap post-processing gimmicks (no random bloom, no chromatic aberration spam, no excessive film grain), V35 establishes a disciplined **5-Material Physical System**. Every visual component in the film must be fabricated from this material taxonomy.

---

## 1. The 5 Canonical Materials

### Material 1: Milled Architectural Brass (`#D4AF37`, `#F3E5AB`, `#996515`)
- **Semantic Role:** The structural skeleton of geometry and the primary vehicle of Persian calligraphy. Represents precision, sacred geometry, and celestial light.
- **Visual & Surface Properties:** 
  - Dual-contour construction: razor-sharp specular outer edge (`#FFF4D2`), warm core gradient (`#D4AF37` $\to$ `#C59B27`), and a deep shadow bevel (`#5A3E0B`).
  - High directional specular response: reacts as if illuminated by a key light positioned at top-right (-35° elevation, +45° azimuth).
- **Depth & Layering:** Casts crisp, directional ambient-occlusion shadows onto supporting planes (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` with tight 6–12px radius, not fuzzy radial glow).
- **Where it appears:** The dividing horizon blade, the iris aperture mechanical blades, the sculpted relief faces of «نقطه دید», and the sacred Nuqteh diamond.

### Material 2: Polished Obsidian / Deep Basalt (`#06080D`, `#0D1117`, `#161B22`)
- **Semantic Role:** The architectural foundation and negative-space substrate. Represents the primordial void from which light and geometry emerge.
- **Visual & Surface Properties:**
  - Non-emitting, deep velvety absorption with subtle anisotropic specular rim glancing.
  - Surface micro-structure: multi-step linear depth steps with hairline etched coordinates (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Depth & Layering:** Exists as background monolithic slabs, recessed ocular chambers, and occluding foreground portals.
- **Where it appears:** The deep background floor, the inner void of the pupil, the architectural colonnade pillars flanking the typography.

### Material 3: Smoked Translucent Silica / Optical Quartz (`rgba(72, 202, 228, 0.08)` to `rgba(240, 246, 252, 0.22)`)
- **Semantic Role:** The optical medium and atmospheric lens. Represents sight, observation, and refraction.
- **Visual & Surface Properties:**
  - Semi-translucent panels with polished, light-catching chamfered borders (1px hairline stroke with high refraction index).
  - Permits background geometry to be visible beneath it with controlled tonal attenuation.
- **Depth & Layering:** Placed primarily in the **Foreground** and **Midground** to create multi-plane parallax and spatial occlusion as the camera dollys through.
- **Where it appears:** The floating aperture diaphragm leaves in Beat 02, the floating perspective scrims in Beat 03, and the protective orbital rings in Beat 05.

### Material 4: Illuminated Luminous Filament (`#FFFFFF` core, `#FFE8A3` inner corona)
- **Semantic Role:** The focal energy vector and calligraphic nucleus. Represents pure photons, insight, and the moment of revelation.
- **Visual & Surface Properties:**
  - Ultra-dense white hairline core (1.2px) flanked by a tight, double-layered optical corona.
  - Unlike cheap blur glows, it maintains a crisp geometric vector profile with hard physical edges.
- **Depth & Layering:** Illuminates nearby surfaces through directional reflection bands.
- **Where it appears:** The central gravitational singularity in Beat 01, the inner edge of the iris opening in Beat 02, the focal apex of the Nuqteh in Beat 04, and the radiant heart of «نور» in Beat 05.

### Material 5: Engraved Lathe Guides & Calibrated Microlines (`#2D3748`, `#4A5568`, opacity 0.3–0.5)
- **Semantic Role:** The unseen mathematical matrix of the cosmos. Represents the underlying blueprint and geometric draftsmanship.
- **Visual & Surface Properties:**
  - Extremely fine hairline geometry (0.5px to 0.75px) with rhythmic metric divisions, vernier scales, and angular tick marks.
  - Strictly non-emissive; etched directly into the obsidian substrate.
- **Depth & Layering:** Anchors the background and deep-space layers, giving scale and mechanical purpose to the empty spaces.
- **Where it appears:** Horizon vernier scales in Beat 01, optical degree calibrations in Beat 02, spatial grid coordinate planes in Beat 03, and celestial astrolabe coordinate circles in Beat 05.

## 2. Material Conservation & Transformation Rules

When one element morphs into another, its material properties must not randomly change:
1. **Horizon Blade $\to$ Iris Blades:** The milled brass horizon edge maintains its specular rim and splits into the 8 overlapping brass iris leaves.
2. **Pupil Portal $\to$ Colonnade Void:** The deep obsidian core of the pupil expands to become the spatial void of the Persian architectural hall.
3. **Sculpted Letterforms $\to$ The Nuqteh:** The brass bevels of «دید» collapse into the solid, heavy milled brass block of the Nuqteh.
4. **The Nuqteh $\to$ «نور»:** The single brass dot retains its material identity, scale, and lighting direction, becoming the diacritical crown dot of the colossal word «نور».

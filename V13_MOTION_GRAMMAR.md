## 1. Core Rule: One Primary Camera Curve Per Shot
V13 replaces random or conflicting camera shakes with a disciplined, motivated editorial camera grammar inspired by `video-talkcraft`'s single-curve philosophy:
- **`micro-push`:** Scale $1.00 \to 1.025$ to draw visual attention during core narrative statements.
- **`micro-pull`:** Scale $1.03 \to 1.00$ to widen view and settle onto the grand resolution.
- **`slow-dolly`:** Smooth horizontal translation ($x_1 \to x_2$) following timeline progression or incoming batons.
- **`parallax-drift`:** Differential depth movement ($z = 1200\text{px}$) separating foreground elements from background datum lines.
- **`punch-in`:** Harmonic micro-accentuation ($+0.025$) on exact syllable impact frames.

## 2. The 4-Role Element Filter (Anti-Noise Invariant)
Every on-screen entity must strictly belong to one of four functional roles:
1. **[A] Narrative:** Directly conveys canonical script facts (headlines, numbers, legal articles).
2. **[B] Structural:** Visualizes relationships and frameworks (plinths, diagrams, dividers, framing brackets).
3. **[C] Kinetic:** Acts as a motive vehicle for state change or transition continuity (carry baseline, baton impulse).
4. **[D] Atmospheric:** Provides subtle architectural depth without drawing focus (faint 80px background grid).

*Any element failing this filter is deleted.*

## 4. Multi-Level Carry Transitions
Transitions in V13 are not decorative wipes; they preserve physical and semantic continuity across boundaries:
- **Level 1 (Object Carry):** An entity physically travels across the cut (e.g. Shot 01 golden underline $\to$ Shot 02 legal numeral apex).

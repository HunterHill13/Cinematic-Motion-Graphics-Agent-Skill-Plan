# VISUAL_CHOREOGRAPHY.md — Scene-by-Scene Visual Choreography Plan (V6)

This document specifies the exact visual concept, metaphor, and graphic transformation for each shot of the film.

---

## Shot 1: Institutional Opening & Core Hook (0 – 380f)
- **Narration:** روابط عمومی کمیته تحقیقات دانشگاه علوم پزشکی بقیه‌الله تقدیم می‌کند... آیا دانشجوی نمونه یا پژوهشگر برتر هستید؟
- **Visual Concept:** The Golden Compass & Identity Genesis.
- **Choreography:**
  - `0–40f`: Concentric reticle rings draw on with clockwise path rotation. The Baqiyatallah PR emblem forms at the center.
  - `40–145f`: Institutional title appears via kinetic mask reveal.
  - `145–180f`: The central seal contracts slightly and radiates an energetic horizon line (`#38BDF8`). The hook question reveals with letter-spacing tracking expansion.
  - `335–380f (Handoff T1)`: The horizon line expands into a full-width **Vector Energy Beam** that shoots forward across the screen to anchor Shot 2.

## Shot 2: Directive Decree of Section Kaf, Article 2 (350 – 650f)
- **Narration:** طبق بند «ک» ماده ۲ آیین‌نامه ارتقای اعضای هیئت علمی و مصوبات وزارت بهداشت...
- **Visual Concept:** The Legal Highway & Branching Nodes.
- **Choreography:**
  - `0–45f`: Inherits the Vector Energy Beam from Shot 1. The beam bifurcates into top and bottom architectural rails.
  - `45–140f`: The central legal decree pill locks into place. A vertical caliper measures out the legal reference points ("بند ک", "ماده ۲").
  - `140–245f`: Two graphic data nodes form along the beam, displaying the Ministry of Health accreditation and University Senate decree.
  - `245–290f (Handoff T2)`: The two nodes stretch vertically into 3 distinct energy columns that form the pillars for Shot 3.

## Shot 3: Three Fundamental Conditions (620 – 1490f)
- **Narration:** برای استفاده از این امتیاز، سه شرط اصلی لازم است: حداقل معدل ۱۶، سنوات مجاز و تأییدیه انضباطی، و کسب امتیاز از حداقل ۶ ماده مختلف...
- **Visual Concept:** The Three Interlocking Keys / Structural Trifold (NOT generic web cards).
- **Choreography:**
  - `0–40f`: The 3 vertical energy pillars settle into 3 open typographic bays with gold/cyan structural guide lines.
  - `40–180f (Condition 1)`: Massive numeric graphic **«۱۶»** (72px Black) assembles from vector tick marks. Caliper locks: «حداقل معدل کل ۱۶».
  - `180–400f (Condition 2)`: Second bay activates. A circular clock gate with legal seal locks: «سنوات مجاز و تأییدیه انضباطی».
  - `400–810f (Condition 3)`: Third bay activates. An interlocking 6-segment polygon forms, with the center lighting up for "مقاله و فناوری": «حداقل ۶ ماده».
  - `810–860f (Handoff T3)`: The 3 bays compress along the horizontal axis, merging into a single temporal gate that enters Shot 4.

## Shot 4: Time Window & 1-Year Limit (1460 – 1730f)
- **Narration:** توجه داشته باشید که این فرصت تنها در طول دوره تحصیل یا حداکثر تا یک سال پس از فراغت از تحصیل معتبر است...
- **Visual Concept:** The Legal Chronometer & Temporal Boundary Gate.
- **Choreography:**
  - `0–40f`: Inherits the merged gate from Shot 3, unfolding into a continuous horizontal timeline axis with start/graduation calipers.
  - `40–160f`: A dynamic glowing highlight sweeps from "دوران تحصیل" to "+۱ سال پس از فراغت". A 365-day arc gauge rotates to lock the deadline.
  - `160–215f`: An amber warning boundary marker lights up to emphasize the strict cutoff.
  - `215–260f (Handoff T4)`: The horizontal timeline compresses its endpoints inward, collapsing into three vertical benchmark needles that sprout into Shot 5.

## Shot 5: Passing Thresholds Across University Tiers (1700 – 2185f)
- **Narration:** حداقل امتیاز لازم برای دانشجویان کارشناسی ۶۵، دکتری عمومی ۱۱۰، و مقاطع تخصصی و پی‌اچ‌دی ۱۳۰ امتیاز است...
- **Visual Concept:** Kinetic Numeric Monoliths & Dynamic Bar Gauges (NOT cards).
- **Choreography:**
  - `0–40f`: Three vertical coordinate lines rise from the floor.
  - `40–140f (Tier 1)`: Giant numeric counter **«۶۵»** builds with cyan energy; dynamic vertical gauge fills up to 65.
  - `140–260f (Tier 2)`: Center numeric counter **«۱۱۰»** builds with gold energy; gauge surges to 110.
  - `260–420f (Tier 3)`: Right numeric counter **«۱۳۰»** builds with emerald energy; gauge surges to 130.
  - `420–475f (Handoff T5)`: The three numbers converge inward toward $(960, 540)$, spinning their vector energy into the central seal for Shot 6.
